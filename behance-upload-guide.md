## 4. Project description (paste as-is, or trim to taste)

Fortress is a rental-property and net-worth tracking app designed for New Zealand landlords running a handful of rooms — too small for enterprise property-management software, too complex for a plain spreadsheet.

**Role:** Solo product designer
**Tools:** Figma Pro + Claude (Figma MCP)
**Scope:** 7 screens, 1 wired prototype, 9 working days

Meet the user: Ben Sullivan, a 31-year-old landlord juggling a day job with a growing handful of rooms, chasing rent over text messages with no single view of what's actually working.

The process: empathy mapping and a persona grounded the problem, a shallow 13-screen information architecture kept everything within 3 taps, and a forest-green + gold design system (built on Fraunces + Inter type) gave Fortress its own identity — distinct from generic fintech blue.

Every screen ties back to one number: a projected financial-freedom date, visualized on a hand-drawn net worth trend chart.

What makes this case study different: every frame, variable, component, and prototype link was created by Claude executing real Figma Plugin API code through the Figma MCP connector — not a human dragging rectangles. 9 real API gotchas were discovered and documented along the way. I directed brand and scope judgment; Claude operated the tool.
